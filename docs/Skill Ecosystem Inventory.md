Updated during the Skill Lifecycle retrofit phase that brought 6 of the original 12 Skills' stubs (`architecture-review`, `database-review`, `performance-review`, `security-review`, `testing-review`, and `api-design`, the last split into two) forward to structural and behavioral-evidence compliance with `docs/Skill Standard.md`, and corrected two Skills (`context-discovery`, `mentor-development`) that were content-mature but structurally non-compliant with the Standard's current frontmatter/section requirements. ## 3. Certification Status — Explicitly Withheld This Phase

The 7 newly-written/retrofitted Review- and Authoring/Workflow-type Skills (`architecture-review`, `database-review`, `performance-review`, `security-review`, `testing-review`, `api-review`, `api-contract-design`) each received a structured review this phase against `docs/Skill Quality Standard.md`'s ten dimensions (scores recorded in the retrofit phase's final report, item 5) — no dimension scored below 3. **None is marked Certified.** Two reasons apply to all 7, stated explicitly per `docs/Skill Quality Standard.md` Section 3 condition 3 rather than silently carried:

1. 2. The review performed this phase was self-review by this Skill's own author in the same session — a real review against the rubric, not a rubber stamp, but not the independence a Reviewed-stage sign-off ideally has. `docs/Skill Taxonomy.md` Section 7 stage 5 requires certification be "formally signed off by whoever approves the change" — that sign-off has not occurred.

These Skills are at **Reviewed** (stage 4): structurally valid, behaviorally evidenced with category-diverse fixtures, and scored against the Quality Standard with no blocking dimension — a real, verifiable step past Tested, short of the independent sign-off Certified requires.

**Phase 11 update (Review Skill Governance Evidence).** Phase 10's re-scoring found that `security-review`, `database-review`, and `api-review` each had only one governance-relationship fixture (a Prohibited Override case), short of `docs/Skill Testing Standard.md` Section 2's full governance-relationship series required for any Review-type Skill — a genuine hard-gate shortfall, not a scored-but-passable weakness (Section 3 of that Standard). ## 8. Phase 15 — Certification Process & Independent Reviewer Readiness (Process Design, Not Certification)

**This phase did not modify any of the three Skills' content**, did not perform another Tier D pilot, and **did not certify anything.** `docs/Skill Taxonomy.md` Section 7 and `docs/Skill Quality Standard.md` Section 3 each received one small, clearly-labeled "Proposed clarification" pointing to the new Process document, without altering either document's existing requirements — the certification threshold and lifecycle stages are exactly as demanding as before this phase. `docs/Skill Testing Standard.md` Section 1 received a similar pointer for Tier D/E's now-concrete (but still Proposed) minimum requirements. **All three target Skills remain at Reviewed.** The Independence Flag (Section 2, above) is unchanged: designing a process that *could* produce a genuinely independent sign-off is not the same act as that sign-off actually occurring, and this phase did not manufacture one.

**What this closes, and what it doesn't.** It closes the process-definition gap Phase 14 identified — an independent Reviewer now has a concrete role definition, independence test, checklist, record schema, and self-contained packet to work from, rather than nothing. It does not close the Independence Flag itself, which can only be closed by an actual independent party doing the actual work the new packets describe. `docs/Skill Certification Process.md` Section 3 records one concrete, currently-available path: the project owner personally executing a packet's checklist against a Skill's actual current files, in their own words, independently of this session's conclusions, would constitute genuine first Tier E evidence — this phase did not perform that on the project owner's behalf, since doing so would defeat its own independence requirement.
